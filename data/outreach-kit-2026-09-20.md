## The message (short, personal, one link)

Subject: **Quote requests with a budget attached — free listing for {Agency}**

> Hi {first name},
>
> I run projectcostestimator.com, an independent website cost calculator (we don't build sites). People price their project on it, then ask for quotes: the request comes with the type, platform, market, size, budget band and deadline already filled in.
>
> I'm listing a small number of {platform} agencies that serve {market}, and {Agency} fits — {one specific thing you saw on their site: a project, a niche, a platform}.
>
> The listing is free, no commission, no contract; requests are routed to listed agencies at no charge right now. If it's useful, the form takes two minutes: https://projectcostestimator.com/for-agencies
>
> Florin Florea
> founder, Project Cost Estimator

Rules that keep it honest and keep replies coming:
- One specific line about *their* site (a case study, a platform badge, a niche). No template feel, no flattery.
- Never a number we don't have: no "X leads a month". The truth is on the page: 37 estimates run in the last 30 days (live counter), 5 agencies listed, requests routed at no charge today.
- The Agency plan ($49/mo: matched first, featured card, Embed Pro) is on the page; don't sell it in the first message.
- If they ask "how many requests will I get": "We don't promise a number; the live figures are on /for-agencies and the listing is free."
- LinkedIn variant (280 chars): "Hi {name} — I run projectcostestimator.com, an independent cost calculator. Buyers price a project, then ask for quotes with the budget attached. Listing a few {platform} agencies in {market}, free, no commission. {Agency} fits — details: projectcostestimator.com/for-agencies"

## Follow-up (one, after 5–7 days)

> Hi {first name}, one follow-up and then I'll leave it: the free listing at projectcostestimator.com/for-agencies is still open for {platform} agencies in {market}. Two minutes, no commission. If it's not for you, no reply needed.
